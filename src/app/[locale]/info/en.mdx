<Heading>GAME PROCEDURES.</Heading>
<Divider />

- All maps will be scored with **osu! Lazer default scoring**.
- **NoFail** will be enforced.
- Referees will contact the Captains of any participating team for each game.
  - Captains are responsible for relaying any information given by the referee to their team.
  - A reminder will be sent out **15 minutes** before each game.
  - Lazer room name and password will be sent out **10 minutes** before each game.
- After a map has been selected, participating players have **2 minutes** to join the lobby.

<Divider variant="single" />

<Heading sub>QUALIFIERS</Heading>

<div className="px-7 md:px-9 lg:px-11">

- Maps will be played in the same order as seen on the mappool.
- If a player **disconnects** **within the first minute of a map**, the player will replay the map at the **end of the qualifier room**.
- Teams are expected to have 3 players in the room within **5 minutes after their room start time**. If a team fails to show up then the team will have to reschedule their room. This room must be at least **4 hours** after the room they failed to show up to.
- Once qualifiers have concluded, teams will be seeded into the top 32 using z-sum as a calculation method.

</div>

<Divider variant="single" />

<Heading sub>MATCH</Heading>

<div className="px-7 md:px-9 lg:px-11">

- The win-condition for each match will be **TeamVS**.
- Warmups, Protects, Bans, and Picks must be submitted within **2 minutes**.
- Teams are expected to have 3 players in the room within **5 minutes after their match start time**. They will forfeit the following at each timestamp.
  - 5 minutes late is forfeit of protects.
  - 10 minutes late is forfeit of bans.
  - 15 minutes late is a forfeit of match.
- Players must be in a lobby slot corresponding to their team.
  - Team 1 (Red): Slots 1, 2, and 3.
  - Team 2 (Blue): Slots 4, 5, and 6.
- Captains will use the **!roll command in the osu chat** to decide **Pick/Ban order**. The captain with the higher roll may select one of the following, while the other captain will receive the other:
  - First to Pick and Second to Ban
  - Second to Pick and First to Ban
- Teams may opt into playing 1 map of their choice as a warmup before the match.
  - Warmups must be **less than 4 minutes long**.
  - Warmups may **not be part of the mappool**.
- Protects will be **privately submitted** to the referee before the banning phase.
- Banning phase will be done in a **snake format** (ABBA).
  - Team 1 Bans
  - Team 2 Bans twice
  - Team 1 Bans
- Once the ban and protect phase has ended, **any bans on a protected map will be voided** and that map will be playable as normal.
- Banning from the **same modpool twice in a row is prohibited** excluding the **No-Mod pool**.
- Picking from the **same modpool twice in a row is prohibited** excluding the **Lazer-Mod** and **No-Mod pools**.
- Players may play Tiebreaker with any mod that is **not a global rate change mod**.
- Each team has **one 5-minute timeout** which may be used at any point in the match.
- If a player **disconnects within the first minute of a map**, the map will be **aborted** and the team has **5 minutes** to field another player to play the map. This may only occur once per team and per match.

</div>
